How it works.

Runs on the box

Every experiment runs in the browser, served from the school's own box, and keeps working with the internet unplugged.

Four depths

Explore, Learn, Experiment and Advanced: the same experiment, from playing around to the maths behind it.

An AI Guide

It reads the experiment's numbers and suggests what to try next. It cannot move the controls: you do.

The same safety

Every question to the Guide goes through the same safety checks as the tutor, and worrying messages reach the school's trusted adult.
