The AI tutor

The AI tutor runs on your box. It is built to help students learn: in homework it gives hints rather than answers, and in assessments it stays out. Every message is screened before and after the tutor answers, and messages suggesting a student may be in danger go to your designated safety contacts.

Even so, AI can be wrong, incomplete or misunderstood, and safety screening cannot catch everything. The tutor is a learning aid, not a replacement for teachers, counsellors or professional advice. Teachers remain responsible for teaching, marking and supervising, and your institution remains responsible for safeguarding.
