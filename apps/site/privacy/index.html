Who we are

EDU54 ("we", "us") makes and sells EDU54, an AI learning lab in one box for schools, colleges and universities. It is built on Umanah Institute LabOS. This policy explains what personal information we handle, why, and the choices you have.

For anything about privacy, email privacy@edu54.com. We aim to reply within 7 days and will answer every request within the time the law allows (usually one month).

Two kinds of data

It helps to separate two things, because the rules are different for each.

1. Data on a school's box

When a school uses EDU54, the names, usernames, conversations with the AI tutor, lessons, assignments, projects, progress and safety alerts of its students and staff are stored on the box at that school. The box does not send them to us, and the tutor, lessons and library run without the internet.

For that data the school is in charge (in law, the "data controller"). The school decides who has an account, what is kept and for how long, and answers requests from students and parents. If you are a student, parent or staff member with a question about that data, please contact your school first. Our Data protection page explains how the box helps schools meet their duties.

2. Data we handle ourselves

We handle a small amount of personal information directly: when you visit this website, try a demo, contact us, or when a school orders a box and we set it up and support it. The rest of this policy is about that data.

Support access to a school's box

We install and configure each box before it ships. After that we only access a box when the school asks us to or agrees, only for as long as the work needs, and only to do that work.

  • We do not open, read or copy student conversations, student work or safety alerts, including during support.
  • If a problem cannot be fixed without touching personal data, we explain why and get the school's written agreement first.
  • Admin actions on the box are recorded in an audit log the school can read.

Legal grounds

Where data protection law (such as the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, Ghana's Data Protection Act 2012, Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019, South Africa's POPIA, or the EU and UK GDPR) asks us to name a legal ground, we rely on:

  • Contract: to quote, sell, deliver, set up and support a box.
  • Legitimate interests: to run and secure this website and the demos, and to answer people who contact us. We weigh these against your rights and keep the data to a minimum.
  • Legal obligation: to keep invoices and tax records.
  • Consent: if we ever want to use data for anything else, such as a case study naming your school, we will ask first, and you can withdraw consent at any time.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share only what is needed with:

  • Service providers who help us run the business: Cloudflare (website delivery and security), our web server host, and our email host. They may only use the data to provide their service to us.
  • Delivery, banking and payment providers when a box is ordered, paid for and shipped.
  • Authorities when the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.
  • A buyer or successor if EDU54 is reorganised or sold, under the same protections.

No third party receives student data from the box through us.

International transfers

Our website and email providers may process data in countries other than yours, including countries outside Africa. When that happens we use providers that offer recognised safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, as required by the Nigeria Data Protection Act, the GDPR and similar laws. Student data on a school's box stays on that box.

How long we keep it

  • Demo copies: deleted within 24 hours, and all demos are reset every day.
  • Website connection logs: kept only as long as needed for security and fault-finding, then deleted.
  • Enquiries: up to 2 years after our last contact, unless they lead to an order.
  • Order, invoice and support records: for as long as we support the box, and after that for as long as tax and company law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it, or limit how we use it;
  • stop using it for a purpose you object to;
  • give it to you, or another organisation, in a common format;
  • withdraw consent you gave earlier.

Email privacy@edu54.com. We may need to confirm who you are. There is no charge. For data on a school's box, ask the school; we will help the school respond.

You can also complain to your data protection authority, for example the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, Ghana's Data Protection Commission, Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, South Africa's Information Regulator, or the authority in your EU country or the UK. We would be grateful for the chance to put things right first.

Children

This website and the demos are meant for adults choosing a product for their institution. Children use EDU54 through their school, on the school's own box, with accounts the school creates. Student accounts need only a display name and a username: no student email address or date of birth is required.

Schools are responsible for any parent or guardian consent the law requires for their students. Our Data protection page explains this for Nigeria, Ghana and other countries.

Security

We protect the information we hold with access controls, encrypted connections (HTTPS) on this website, private networks for support, and by keeping as little as possible. On the box, everyone signs in with their own account, each role sees only what it needs, and admin actions are recorded. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal information, we will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires (in Nigeria and under the GDPR, the authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it).

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and show the new date at the top. If a change matters for schools that have bought a box, we will tell them directly. Questions: privacy@edu54.com.
