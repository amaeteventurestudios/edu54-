Questions schools ask.

What heads, owners and bursars ask before they buy.

How much does EDU54 cost?

Each box is quoted per order, based on the edition and the size of your school or campus. You pay for the box and its setup. There is no monthly subscription and no per-student fee.

How many students can use one box?

One box serves a whole class at the same time through their browsers. Boxes come in different sizes for a classroom, a whole school or a campus. We will publish exact capacity figures once they are measured on the final hardware.

What happens if the power goes out?

The box needs power like any computer. Saved work and lesson progress are stored on the box itself, so they are still there when power returns. We recommend a small backup power supply (UPS) to ride through short outages.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The tutor, lessons and library all run on the box at the school, so core learning continues with the internet unplugged.

Does EDU54 support local curricula?

Classes use your levels (Primary 1 to SSS 3 by default, or Year 1 to Postgraduate). The built-in courses cover AI, Python and science and engineering. Teachers add their own lessons and materials so the box follows what the class is actually taught. The built-in courses are not yet mapped to a national curriculum.

Can teachers upload their own lessons and materials?

Yes. Teachers upload PDFs, pictures, text files and EPUB books for their students, and can draft lessons with the AI lesson builder, edit them and publish them to their class.

Won't the AI just do their homework?

No. In homework the tutor gives hints, not answers, and in assessments it stays out. These limits are enforced by the software on the box, not only requested in a prompt.

Can students use it from home?

Not by default. The box serves your school's own network, which keeps conversations on campus. Students use it on site, from the devices they already have.

Does EDU54 support multiple languages?

English today. Every screen is built to be translated, and French and Portuguese are planned next.

How do updates work without internet?

The box keeps working offline for as long as you need. New software and content are installed by us; an automatic update process is on our roadmap.

What happens if the box breaks?

Your school's data can be backed up and exported from the admin area. Repair and support terms are agreed with each order.

Can school administrators monitor student progress?

Yes. Teachers see class progress, assignments and students who may need help. Staff can look across the school by level, class and student, and admins manage people, classes and settings.

How does EDU54 protect student information?

Student data stays on your school's box and is not sent to us. Everyone signs in with their own account, and each role sees only what it needs. Safety alerts go only to your designated safety contacts, and admin actions are recorded in an audit log.

Can we try EDU54 before buying?

Yes. Open the K-12 or University demo on this page right now: no sign-up, made-up students and sample data.
