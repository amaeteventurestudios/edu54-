# Teacher Center

## Purpose

Give educators control over classes, curriculum, assignments, student progress, revision, assessment, resources, and AI-assisted planning.

## Overview screen

For a selected class show:
- enrollment count;
- lesson completion states;
- average completion;
- students needing attention based on transparent learning signals;
- recent submissions;
- fast teacher actions.

## Classes

Roster, enrollment, course assignment, class settings, schedule metadata, aggregate progress.

## Curriculum

Browse Umanah pathways, teacher-created content, approved resources, and assignable lessons. Teachers can clone/adapt content subject to licensing and school policy.

## Assignments

Create from curriculum or scratch. Define due date, instructions, resources, rubric, AI-help policy, scoring, and allowed tools.

## Revision

Generate or assign topic packs based on completed work. Students should never see hidden teacher diagnostic labels unless intended.

## Assessments

Quizzes, answer keys, scoring, retakes, accommodations, integrity/help policy.

## AI Lesson Builder

Inputs: objectives, grade/year, duration, standards/competencies, available resources, differentiation needs. Output is a draft requiring teacher review before publication.

## Teacher authority

AI suggestions are advisory. Teacher can override recommended revision, grading suggestions, and generated materials.

## Hint ladders and answer keys

When creating or adapting practice and homework, teachers see and can edit each question's hint ladder and answer key (`42`). The AI Lesson Builder drafts them; the teacher approves before they go live.

## Seeing student AI chats

Teachers can view tutor chats linked to their assignments and lessons, per the school's setting (`41`). Free Ask chats are private to the student by default.

## Safety alerts

Safety alerts go to the school's designated safety contacts, not to every teacher (`41`). A teacher who is also a designated contact sees the Safety Alerts view.
