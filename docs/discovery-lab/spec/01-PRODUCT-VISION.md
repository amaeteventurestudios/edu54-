# 01 Product vision

Discovery Lab is a hands-on library, not another mandatory grade-level curriculum. **Discovery Lab → Laboratory → Collection → Topic → Interactive Experience**. Let any age browse; adapt explanation and controls with four optional depth modes: **Explore, Learn, Experiment, Advanced**. Grade, curriculum standards and prerequisite mapping stay behind the scenes.

Core journeys: free sandbox exploration; guided lesson with objective, steps, measurements and assessment; teacher launch from existing K–12/university course; self-directed inquiry with the AI Guide.

Success: 13 subject labs; fast offline cross-lab discovery; scientifically sound controllable simulations; progress and assignments integrated where existing platform supports them; clean and animated UI matching the current site; commercial compliance; offline LAN operation.

Use one underlying simulation with configurable presets for younger and older learners rather than copying it into different curriculums. Keep “Working”, “Prototype”, “Planned” statuses honest.
