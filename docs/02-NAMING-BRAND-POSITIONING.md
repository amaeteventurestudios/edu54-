# Naming, Brand, and Positioning

> Shared project facts, brand rules, agent rules and skills live in one place each: `00-START-HERE.md` (facts), `06-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` (visual), `../CLAUDE.md` (agent contract), `34-CLAUDE-SKILLS-SETUP.md` (skills). This document does not repeat them.

## Naming hierarchy

### Umanah Institute
The nonprofit organization and institutional parent.

### Umanah AI Lab
The public program/product. This is what schools, donors, students, teachers, press, and partners should remember.

### Umanah LabOS
The underlying orchestration and appliance software platform. It is infrastructure branding, not the default hero headline.

### Deployment sizes
- **AI Lab Mini** — classroom, library, community center, pilot.
- **AI Lab School** — school-wide/multi-classroom.
- **AI Lab Research** — university/nonprofit research/high-performance.

## Canonical phrases

- “Umanah AI Lab, powered by Umanah LabOS.”
- “Local AI infrastructure for classrooms, libraries, and community learning environments.”
- “Locally independent. Centrally supported.”
- “We monitor the machine. We do not monitor the child.”
- “One appliance. Many students.”
- “AI access without the cloud bill.” may be used selectively, but avoid sounding anti-cloud or simplistic.

## Positioning update (v2)

Lead with offline, privacy, ownership and learning; cost is a supporting point. See `48-LANDSCAPE-POSITIONING.md`.

## Words to avoid

Avoid describing the product primarily as:
- a ChatGPT clone;
- a Moodle wrapper;
- an Open WebUI installation;
- a Raspberry Pi project;
- a low-income-only product;
- a surveillance/monitoring platform;
- an “AI replaces teachers” system.

## Audience positioning

### Schools
Private, shared AI and technical learning infrastructure with predictable local control.

### Libraries/community centers
A shared learning resource that can serve many patrons from one local appliance.

### Sponsors
A concrete infrastructure unit that can be funded, deployed, measured, and reported.

### Teachers
Structured AI-assisted learning with teacher authority and guardrails.

### Students
A place to ask, learn, build, explore, and see progress.

## Brand governance

New product names, feature labels, or deployment tier names should not be invented casually. Prefer descriptive feature language inside the established hierarchy. Record any approved naming change in the decision log.
