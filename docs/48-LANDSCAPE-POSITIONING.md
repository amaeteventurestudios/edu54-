## Why this matters

Sponsors and school leaders will ask, "Why not just use Google's or Microsoft's AI?" and "Isn't this like Kolibri?" The pitch must answer both honestly. Details about other products change often; check current terms before quoting them publicly.

## What else exists (as of September 2026)

### Cloud AI for schools

- **Google Gemini for Education / Gemini in Classroom** — since mid-2025, included in all Google Workspace for Education editions at no extra cost, with paid upgrades available.
- **Microsoft Copilot for education**, **OpenAI ChatGPT Edu** (mainly universities), **Khan Academy's Khanmigo**, **MagicSchool** and others — teacher tools and student tutors, delivered from the cloud.

What they share: they need reliable internet, student data is processed by the provider, and access depends on the provider's terms and pricing over time.

### Offline education servers (no AI tutor)

- **Kolibri** (Learning Equality) — open-source, offline-first learning platform with sync; widely used in low-connectivity settings.
- **RACHEL** (World Possible) and **Internet-in-a-Box** — offline content servers (Wikipedia, Khan Academy videos, textbooks) for schools without internet.
- **Kiwix** — offline reader for Wikipedia and other libraries.

### Local AI tools (not school products)

- **Ollama**, **llama.cpp**, **Open WebUI** — run models locally; no curriculum, teacher control or school management.
- **Chatty-EDU** — offline education-focused local AI for Windows desktops (`09`).

## Where Umanah AI Lab is different

No single product above combines all of these:

1. a **local AI tutor** that keeps working without internet;
2. **structured curriculum** the tutor is grounded in, not a blank chat box;
3. **teacher control** over when and how AI helps (hints-only homework enforced by the system);
4. **built-in child safety** that alerts the school, not a vendor;
5. **student data stays on the school's own box**;
6. a **managed appliance** — plug in, enroll, supported remotely without being dependent on the remote side.

## How to pitch it now

Cloud AI is increasingly free to schools, so "no subscription fees" can't be the headline. Lead with what cloud AI can't offer:

| Lead with | Say it like |
|---|---|
| Works without internet | "Learning doesn't stop when the connection does." |
| Data stays at the school | "Student conversations never leave the building." |
| The school owns it | "Your AI, on your premises, under your policies." |
| Built for learning, not chatting | "A tutor that knows the lesson and gives hints, not answers." |
| Real AI literacy | "Students learn how AI works on a machine they can see." |
| Predictable cost | "One box, many students, no per-student fees." (supporting point) |

Avoid sounding anti-cloud. The honest framing: cloud AI is a good fit for some schools; Umanah is for places where connectivity, privacy, ownership or cost rules it out.

## Claims rules

- Compare only on facts we can cite with a date.
- Never say a competitor "sells student data" or similar unless their own documents say so.
- Don't claim learning gains until measured properly (`37`).
