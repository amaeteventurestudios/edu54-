# Fundraising and Sponsorship Strategy

## Initial target

**$100,000** to establish the central development lab, finish the first platform generation, and deploy the first set of AI Labs.

## Illustrative allocation (updated for Mac mini pricing)

A site kit (Mac mini 64 GB / 1 TB, UPS, access point, cables, shipping) is about **$3,500** (`51`), not the ~$2,000 assumed in v1. Two options; **owner decision** which to pitch.

| Category | A: $30K central lab | B: $15K central lab |
|---|---:|---:|
| Central development & test lab | $30,000 | $15,000 |
| AI Lab site kits @ ~$3,500 | $28,000 (8 sites) | $42,000 (12 sites) |
| Software / platform development | $15,000 | $15,000 |
| Curriculum & teacher materials | $6,000 | $6,000 |
| Safeguarding, privacy & legal review | $4,000 | $4,000 |
| Installation & teacher training | $5,000 | $6,000 |
| First-year support & monitoring | $6,000 | $7,000 |
| Program evaluation | $2,000 | $2,000 |
| Contingency | $4,000 | $3,000 |
| **Total** | **$100,000** | **$100,000** |

What the central lab contains: one or two Mac minis in the exact field configuration (the reference for benchmarks and support), a high-memory machine for larger models and teacher-content generation, network switch and access points matching school setups, UPS, backup storage, and a Chromebook/tablet test fleet.

Treat this as planning, not an audited grant budget.

## Story

Do not pitch “buy us an expensive Mac.” Pitch: **establish a development/test lab and deploy the first generation of local AI learning systems into real communities.**

## Sponsor units

- **Sponsor an AI Lab** — around $6,500: one lab plus its first year of support and teacher training.
- **Sponsor a Lab for Three Years** — around $12,500: one lab, three years of support and updates.
- **Sponsor a School** — around $20,000: two labs, two years of support.
- **Founding Technology Partner** — $25,000 and up.

Finalize after BOM validation.

## Corporate outreach

The existing sponsor directory includes contacts at relevant technology/infrastructure companies. Use contacts as door-openers to education, CSR, foundation, social-impact, workforce, hardware donation, or community teams—not indiscriminate mass outreach.

## Hardware donation program

Accept validated retiring Mac minis/workstations/GPUs/network/storage where refurbishment economics and reliability make sense. This adds digital-equity and e-waste-reduction value.
