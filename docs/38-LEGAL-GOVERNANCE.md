# Legal and Governance Framework

## Entity separation

Umanah Institute and USG are distinct. Umanah AI Lab is positioned as a Umanah Institute public-interest initiative.

## Nonprofit asset governance

Institute-funded equipment remains Institute property. If central lab equipment is located in a founder’s home, maintain board authorization, asset inventory/serials, receipts, insurance, documented nonprofit use, equipment/facility-use policy, and conflict-of-interest controls.

## Related-party transactions

Any USG ↔ Umanah services, licensing, shared resources, or vendor relationship should be written, disclosed, board-reviewed as appropriate, arm’s-length, and supported by fair-market-value reasoning.

## IP policy

Document pre-existing IP separately. Institute-funded program work should have explicit ownership rules. Open-source contributions and licenses should be tracked.

## Policies to prepare

- conflict of interest;
- IP/opensource policy;
- equipment use/home-lab policy;
- privacy/data policy;
- acceptable use;
- pilot participation/site agreement;
- security/incident response;
- records/retention.

## Professional review

Use nonprofit counsel and CPA for high-stakes governance/tax questions and open-source counsel for license architecture as the program moves beyond prototype.
